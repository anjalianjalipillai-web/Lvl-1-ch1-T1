One night, Luna the Moon Robot floated down to Earth and found a big box of photos. "What are these furry things?" Luna wondered.

🐱 🐶 🐈 🐕

Some had pointy ears and whiskers. Some had floppy ears and waggy tails. But Luna had never seen a cat or a dog before!

👧 🖼️ ➡️ 🏷️

A girl named Mia helped. She showed Luna 20 cat photos and said "cat" each time. Then 20 dog photos and said "dog".

🤖 💡 🐶 ✅

The next day Mia held up a brand-new photo. Luna beeped: "That's a DOG!" Luna had learned from examples!

🤔 ❓ 🐈

How did YOU learn what a cat looks like? Did someone give you a rule… or did you see lots and lots of cats?

👩‍🏫 Teacher tip: Stop on the last slide and take 3–4 answers. Bring out the big idea: we learn by seeing many examples — and AI does the same!

🐈‍⬛ An AI only saw orange cats. Will it know a black cat?

📱 Where do you see AI recognising pictures in real life?
Phones that unlock with your face, photo apps that find all your dog pictures, cars that read road signs.
🇪🇺 GDPR · Ask first
🤳 Is it OK to use photos of people to train AI without asking them?
No! Photos of people are personal. In Europe, a law called GDPR says we must ask before using someone's personal data.
